**Follow your agents, and bring them your files.**

The eighth beta of Avala, for Windows and Linux. It is a **prerelease**: expect rough edges, and please report them. Installed with the Windows installer, the portable zip or the AppImage, an earlier beta offers this update in place. There was no 0.1.0-beta.7 release: its work ships here.

## What changed since 0.1.0-beta.6

This release lets you follow every agent an orchestrator runs as closely as its own conversation, move through a long conversation and step back to an earlier attempt, and hand an agent the files, images, commits and jobs it should look at.

### Following your agents

**Every orchestration, live** ([#13](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/13)). The Delegation pane shows each child job as one compact row: what it is doing now, how much context it has left, what it spent and how many sub-agents it runs. Children that need you come first, then running ones; finished ones fold into one "N done" line. Click a row to open its card, one at a time: its last three actions and how long ago they started, its spend against its carve, its tokens and its sub-agents. The orchestrator's card adds its context left, marked "compacted" when its harness compacted it, the tokens of the whole tree and how many children need you, run, are done or were refused. The footer says where each connection compacts a conversation, such as "compacts at 250k".

**Peek** follows a child's conversation inside its card, its latest rows as they arrive, with a permission or a question it asks answerable there and its own composer to send it a message, without leaving the conversation you are in. Click a sub-agent's pill to list the tools it ran, newest first.

**Overview, Delegation** stacks every orchestration at once, most recent activity first, each headed by its title, repository, jobs and agents, how many need you, tokens and spend. **Active** shows the trees still at work or waiting for you; **Finished** and **All** show the rest, finished ones collapsed to their header. Pick a repository or a connection to keep only the trees that touch it.

**A pull request the agent opened is watched** ([#63](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/63)). When the agent pushes its branch and opens the pull request itself, for example with `gh pr create`, Avala finds it when the turn ends and watches its checks and reviews in the inspector's Pull request section, as it does for one opened with Open pull request. Without a `pullRequest` section in `.avala/jobs.json`, the forge connection that hosts `origin` is used, watch-only.

### Moving through a conversation

**Turn by turn** ([#103](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/103)). Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ bring the previous or the next turn to the top, and End jumps back to the latest when you are not typing.

**What each turn cost** ([#104](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/104)). Hover the end of a turn for its tokens by type, input, output, cache read and cache write, the model and the cost of the whole turn. A turn whose harness reported no usage says so instead of "0 tokens".

**Your draft waits for you** ([#105](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/105)). What you type stays in its conversation, with its attachments and mentions, when you switch to another conversation or close Avala, and the sidebar marks a conversation with an unsent draft with a pencil. In an empty composer, ↑ brings back the messages you sent in that conversation, newest first.

**Restore to an earlier attempt** ([#106](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/106)). The last turn of each attempt shows its checkpoint, the files that attempt changed. When the job waits for you, Restore to here returns the worktree to that checkpoint after you confirm. What came after is kept in a checkpoint of its own, so a restore can be undone, files git ignores are left alone, and the composer gets a message telling the agent what changed back.

### Bringing the agent your files

**Attachments** ([#90](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/90)). The composer of a job attaches files by its paperclip, by drop or by paste. A pasted image, or a pasted text over 4,000 characters, becomes an attachment of its own. Attachments show as chips above the draft and under the prompt or message they went with. Claude Code takes PNG, JPEG, GIF and WebP images, PDFs and text files; Avala keeps a copy of each, 20 MB at most, in the data folder's `attachments` folder, with the job's instruction and attempts, so a retry or a resumed job sends them again.

**Mentions with @** ([#91](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/91)). Typing `@` in the composer suggests up to eight files, folders and recent commits of the job's worktree, and your other jobs, each with an icon for its kind. ↑ and ↓ choose, Enter or Tab writes the mention, Esc closes the list. When the message is sent, it ends with a `Referenced:` list saying what each mention still in the text refers to, so the agent knows exactly what you meant.

**Find in a conversation** ([#92](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/92)). Ctrl/⌘+F opens a find bar over the conversation. The rows that match are tinted, the current one more, with "n of m". Enter and Shift+Enter step through the matches, scrolling each into view and unfolding a folded tool row that holds it; Esc closes the bar. Mod+F is listed among the shortcuts.

### Also in this release

**Fixed: sub-agents in the background** ([#95](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/95)). When Claude Code ended its reply while a sub-agent it started in the background was still working, every tool call of that sub-agent was refused with "No turn is running.", and the reply Claude Code wrote once the sub-agent finished never showed. Avala now opens a turn for that work by itself: the sub-agent's tool calls run to the end, and the reply after its notification shows in the conversation.

**Fixed: the Delegation pane flickering.** With the pointer over a card in the Delegation pane, the card's outline blinked on and off as the pane refreshed. The cards now stay in place and update, so the one under the pointer stays still.

**Sub-agents in the Delegation pane** ([#98](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/pull/98)). Every tool a Claude Code sub-agent called used to show up as a row of the conversation. Now a sub-agent keeps one row, and its tools stay out of the conversation; a permission it asks for still shows there. The Delegation pane opens by itself when the job you are viewing starts a sub-agent, even a job that delegated nothing, and once you close it, it opens again only for the next one. Each sub-agent shows what it is running now and a live "N tool uses · X tokens".

**Readable folders** ([#101](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/pull/101)). An agent working in one repository often needs to read others, and every read outside the job's worktree asked, or was denied under Autonomous. A job now reads files in a readable folder without asking, even under Autonomous, and the audit records the built-in rule `reads-in-readable-folders`. By default the readable folder is the projects folder Avala clones repositories into; `readable.json` in the data folder can name others, as `{ "folders": ["~/Projects", "/srv/repositories"] }`. A path counts by its real location, with links followed. Edits there still go to a person, reads anywhere else still ask, and a repository rule still comes first.

**Fixed: a connection counted twice in Usage** ([#99](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/pull/99)). A connection used both in earlier runs and in a live session showed up twice in Usage. It is now counted once, under its live provider.

**Fixed: a project holding only archived conversations could not be deleted** ([#100](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/pull/100)). A repository's project showed empty in the sidebar, yet deleting it asked to move its conversations first, because its archived conversations counted. Archived conversations no longer keep a project from being deleted; the project stays deleted after a restart, and comes back when one of its conversations is restored.

Everything else is as described in the [0.1.0-beta.6 notes](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.6).

## Supported harness

- **Claude Code.** Avala runs the `claude` CLI in its streaming JSON mode, so Claude Code must be installed, with `claude` on the `PATH`, and signed in. Avala finds each login on its own: the default `~/.claude` folder, other `~/.claude-*` folders, the folder in `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, or an API key or token in the environment, and offers a connection for each.

## Check a package before trusting it

`./Avala --version` prints `Avala 0.1.0-beta.8 (<commit>)`, and `./Avala --smoke` starts the real application headless in a temporary data folder and prints `Avala <version> composed <n> pages and showed its main window.` On Windows, pipe them to `Out-Host` in PowerShell.

## Privacy

Unchanged from 0.1.0-beta.1: anonymous usage statistics to [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com), on by default, an `installed` event once and an `active` event at most once per local day, and nothing about your repositories, jobs, prompts, paths, harnesses, connections, accounts, costs or features. Turn it off on the first-run notice, in Settings, About, or with `DO_NOT_TRACK=1` or `AVALA_TELEMETRY=0`. Attachments and drafts stay on your machine; attachments go only to the harness of the job they were sent with.

## Known limitations

- This is a beta prerelease, told about later prereleases as well as stable releases.
- Windows and Linux only for now; macOS packages come later.
- Packages are unsigned.
- Claude Code is the only supported harness.
- No Flatpak on Flathub yet; the Flatpak can be built from the tar.gz.
- The Archived page has no search yet, and nothing is archived automatically.
- A peek shows text files only; images and pages open in their own preview.
- Continue on your phone needs a harness that offers it: today Claude Code signed in with claude.ai. A sub-agent shows what it does and its state, not its type.
- The welcome screen's composer does not take attachments yet; attach them once the job has started.
- Whether Claude Code's own sub-agents compact at their connection's window has not been confirmed yet.
