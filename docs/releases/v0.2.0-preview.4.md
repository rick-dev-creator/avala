**Meet Ava.**

The fourth preview of Avala 0.2, for Windows and Linux. It is a **prerelease**: expect rough edges, and please report them. Installed with the Windows installer, the portable zip or the AppImage, 0.2.0-preview.3 and earlier offer this update in place.

## What changed since 0.2.0-preview.3

### Ava

Avala has a face now. Ava is the one who runs your agents: calm, a little playful, and very hard to fool. She is the app's icon, she greets you on the welcome screen, and she sits with every conversation.

- **Four moods that follow the job.** Focused while it runs, asking when it needs you, worried when a check fails, pleased when it is ready, each with her own portrait and a frame in its colour.
- **One short line, never in the way.** When something happens she says it in a line, word by word. In a wide window she stands in the empty margin beside the conversation; in a narrow one she is a single line above the composer that opens only when a job needs you, fails or is ready, and folds again after six seconds, or as soon as you type or scroll back. The chat never grows because of her.
- **Beside the job's status** in the header, small enough to leave room for everything else.

Her lines are fixed for each moment in this preview; a small model will write them from what happened in a later one.

### A new look

Avala now looks like the interface of a futuristic game, without making a show of it.

- **Seven themes.** Night and Day, the dark and light ones, and Tide, Unit, Ash, Bloom and Signal, chosen in Settings, Appearance, each with a swatch in its own colours.
- **Panels, labels and marks.** Cut corners, square tags in Chakra Petch, status diamonds, a carmine bar on the chosen row and on the open inspector section, the sidebar and the inspector in each theme's own colour.
- **The graphs.** The delegation tree, the connection hubs, the C4 diagrams and the Relays pane of Elastic Harness Chaining take the same look.
- **Motion that means something.** Panels unfold, a failed check glitches once, meters fill segment by segment, the page cursor glides to the page you choose, rows show brackets on hover. Reduced motion, in Settings, Appearance, keeps every end state and none of the movement.

### From a phone's width to a wide desktop

The window now narrows to 360 pixels. Below 1000 the sidebar becomes a drawer over the conversation, below 720 the inspector becomes a sheet over the whole window, and Escape or the scrim closes them.

### Fixes

- **Avala says why it ended.** A signal that stops Avala (SIGTERM, SIGHUP, SIGINT) now stops every agent in order and is logged, and so is any other exit.
- **A pending checks proposal comes back after a restart**, instead of leaving its job in Checking with every message queued.
- **A retried sub-agent is no longer reported out of retries** by an announcement that arrived late.

Everything else is as described in the [0.2.0-preview.3 notes](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/releases/tag/v0.2.0-preview.3).

## Supported harness

- **Claude Code.** Avala runs the `claude` CLI in its streaming JSON mode, so Claude Code must be installed, with `claude` on the `PATH`, and signed in. Avala finds each login on its own: the default `~/.claude` folder, other `~/.claude-*` folders, the folder in `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, or an API key or token in the environment, and offers a connection for each.

Each package is self-contained, and every installer and archive has a `.sha256` checksum next to it. The builds are still unsigned: Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher, and Linux packages are checked against their `.sha256`.

## Check a package before trusting it

`./Avala --version` prints `Avala 0.2.0-preview.4 (<commit>)`, and `./Avala --smoke` starts the real application headless in a temporary data folder and prints `Avala <version> composed <n> pages and showed its main window.` On Windows, pipe them to `Out-Host` in PowerShell.

## Privacy

Unchanged from 0.2.0-preview.3. Anonymous usage statistics go to [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com), on by default: an `installed` event once and an `active` event at most once per local day, with nothing about your repositories, jobs, prompts, paths, harnesses, connections, accounts, costs or features. Turn them off on the first-run notice, in Settings, About, or with `DO_NOT_TRACK=1` or `AVALA_TELEMETRY=0`. A fleet server, only once you connect one in Settings, Fleet, receives what each job does, and nothing else.

## Known limitations

- This is a preview prerelease, told about later prereleases as well as stable releases.
- Windows and Linux only for now; macOS packages come later.
- Packages are unsigned.
- Claude Code is the only supported harness.
- **The fleet server:**
  - registered computers cannot be listed yet;
  - there is no web interface: the Fleet page in Avala is how you read the fleet;
  - only one server may run against a database.
