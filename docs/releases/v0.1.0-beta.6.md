**Follow the agents wherever they work.**

The sixth beta of Avala, for Windows and Linux. It is a **prerelease**: expect rough edges, and please report them. Installed with the Windows installer, the portable zip or the AppImage, an earlier beta offers this update in place.

## What changed since 0.1.0-beta.5

This release lets you follow a job away from your desk, and see how an orchestrator shares out its work without leaving its conversation.

**Continue on your phone** ([#85](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/85)). In the header of a job you started, the phone glyph opens Continue on your phone: turn it on, then scan the QR code or open the link in the Claude app or any browser. Messages you send from there run in the job's conversation, marked "From your phone", and one sent while the job waits for review continues it. Avala still decides: its permission rules, budgets and review apply, a permission request waits in Avala, and Remote Control pauses while Avala holds a job. Only the job you started goes to the phone, never the jobs it delegates. Settings, Claude Code, **Start jobs on your phone** turns it on for every job you start and every chat. It works with Claude Code signed in with claude.ai, through Claude Code's Remote Control, never with an API key.

**The delegation tree beside the conversation** ([#12](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/issues/12)). The tree glyph in the header of a job that delegated, or of one of its children, opens the Delegation pane: the job you started at the top, every child below with its harness, connection, role, activity and spend, the job you are viewing outlined, and the jobs on each connection counted at the foot. Under each job, the sub-agents its harness runs inside its own session show as dashed pills with what they are doing and whether they are running, waiting or done; Overview, Delegation shows them too. The pane takes the preview's place beside the conversation.

**Behind the scenes.** QR codes are drawn with QRCoder, a new dependency.

Everything else is as described in the [0.1.0-beta.5 notes](https://github.com/rick-dev-creator/avala/releases/tag/v0.1.0-beta.5).

## Supported harness

- **Claude Code.** Avala runs the `claude` CLI in its streaming JSON mode, so Claude Code must be installed, with `claude` on the `PATH`, and signed in. Avala finds each login on its own: the default `~/.claude` folder, other `~/.claude-*` folders, the folder in `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, or an API key or token in the environment, and offers a connection for each.

| Arch Linux | `avala-bin-0.1.0-beta.6.PKGBUILD` | the recipe of the AUR package `avala-bin`, built from the tar.gz |

Each package is self-contained, and every installer and archive has a `.sha256` checksum next to it. The builds are still unsigned: Windows SmartScreen warns about an unknown publisher, and Linux packages are checked against their `.sha256`.

## Check a package before trusting it

`./Avala --version` prints `Avala 0.1.0-beta.6 (<commit>)`, and `./Avala --smoke` starts the real application headless in a temporary data folder and prints `Avala <version> composed <n> pages and showed its main window.` On Windows, pipe them to `Out-Host` in PowerShell.

## Privacy

Unchanged from 0.1.0-beta.1: anonymous usage statistics to [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com), on by default, an `installed` event once and an `active` event at most once per local day, and nothing about your repositories, jobs, prompts, paths, harnesses, connections, accounts, costs or features. Turn it off on the first-run notice, in Settings, About, or with `DO_NOT_TRACK=1` or `AVALA_TELEMETRY=0`.

## Known limitations

- This is a beta prerelease, told about later prereleases as well as stable releases.
- Windows and Linux only for now; macOS packages come later.
- Packages are unsigned.
- Claude Code is the only supported harness.
- No Flatpak on Flathub yet; the Flatpak can be built from the tar.gz.
- The Archived page has no search yet, and nothing is archived automatically.
- A peek shows text files only; images and pages open in their own preview.
- Continue on your phone needs a harness that offers it: today Claude Code signed in with claude.ai. A sub-agent shows what it does and its state, not its type.
